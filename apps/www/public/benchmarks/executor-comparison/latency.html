Honesty — ClawQL heavy turn p99 18.21ms stays under the Executor high (100ms) while doing more work.
  • ClawQL arm is deliberately heavier than Executor. Compare p50/p95/p99 on the chart; do not claim apples-to-apples equal work.
  • Executor uses the published warm 50–100ms self-host band until EXECUTOR_BIN/URL is wired.
  • CLAWQL_WORM_ENABLED=1 (memory). search appends durable WORM; execute skips process WORM (by design); audit is ring-buffer only.
  • CLAWQL_CAPABILITY_LIFECYCLE=0 (throughput path). No panguard-mcp-proxy / JWT-ATR hop.
  • p99 needs adequate n; default iters=100. Still sensitive to rare GC/scheduling spikes.
