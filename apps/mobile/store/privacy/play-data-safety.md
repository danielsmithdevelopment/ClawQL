# Google Play Data safety (draft)

**Data collected:**
- Account info (email, name, user id) — app functionality, account management
- App activity (approve/decline events) — app functionality, fraud prevention / security
- Device or other IDs (push token) — app functionality

**Data shared:** Not shared with third parties for advertising. Push delivery uses Apple/Google push infrastructure.

**Security practices:** Data encrypted in transit; users can request deletion in-app (Profile → Delete account).

**Committed to Play Families Policy:** No (not designed for children).
