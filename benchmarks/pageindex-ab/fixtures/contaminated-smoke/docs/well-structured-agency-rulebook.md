# Agency Compliance Rulebook

Published for ClawQL pageindex-ab contaminated-smoke only. Not a real regulation.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This rulebook governs reporting for covered financial institutions. It is intentionally well-structured so heading navigation can succeed.

## 2. Defined terms

### 2.1 Covered institution

A bank, broker-dealer, or investment adviser with more than USD 10 billion in assets under management.

### 2.2 Protocol identifier

The **orchid protocol** identifier for this rulebook is **orchid-77**. Systems must cite orchid-77 in every automated filing.

### 2.3 Reporting cycle

The reporting cycle is quarterly unless the agency grants a written waiver.

## 3. Filing procedures

### 3.1 Electronic submission

Covered institutions submit filings through the Agency Portal. Filings without the protocol identifier are rejected.

### 3.2 Late filings

Late filings incur a base fee of USD 2,500 per business day, capped at USD 50,000 per cycle.

## 4. Retention and audit

### 4.1 Record retention

Institutions must retain source documents for **4.2 years** after the filing date. Electronic copies satisfy this requirement when checksums are preserved.

### 4.2 Audit access

The agency may request audit packages within the retention window. Failure to produce packages within 15 business days is a material deficiency.

## 5. Board governance

### 5.1 Board composition

Each covered institution must maintain a board with **9** directors, including at least three independent directors.

### 5.2 Compensation committee

Compensation committee charters are outside this rulebook. See the separate Compensation Manual.

## 6. Cross-cutting obligations

Obligations in sections 2.2 and 4.1 apply jointly: every retained archive must label packages with orchid-77 and the retention clock of 4.2 years.
