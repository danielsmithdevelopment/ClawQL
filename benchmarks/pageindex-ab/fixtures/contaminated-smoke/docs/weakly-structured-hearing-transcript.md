# Hearing transcript — Committee on Markets (synthetic)

Chair: Good morning. We are here to discuss market structure.

Sen. Hale: Thank you. I want to ask about the clearing outage last March.

Witness Rivera: The outage began at 09:14 Eastern. Root cause was a configuration drift in the matching engine failover.

Chair: How long until recovery?

Witness Rivera: Full recovery at 11:02 Eastern. During the window, about 18 percent of orders were rejected.

Sen. Hale: Was there a customer notification protocol?

Witness Rivera: We posted a status banner within twelve minutes. SMS went out at minute twenty.

[crosstalk]

Sen. Ortiz: I am less interested in banners and more in whether your **incident severity code** for that day was **SEV-2** or SEV-1.

Witness Rivera: Internally we coded it **SEV-2** because clearing resumed under two hours and no trades were broken.

Chair: Moving on to a different topic — lunch break scheduling for staff.

Staff aide: Lunch is at noon in room B.

Sen. Hale: One more market question buried after the lunch digression: the **maximum order cancel rate** observed that morning was **41 percent** at 09:40.

Witness Rivera: That matches our telemetry.

Chair: We will adjourn. No further questions on CEO compensation; that topic is not in this hearing record.
